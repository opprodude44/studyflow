Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who we are

StudyFlow is run by a student developer in Dubai, UAE. Contact email: coming soon.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, first name, birth year, and how you signed in (email, Google, Microsoft or Apple).
  • Your plan: the answers you give during setup (school, subjects, grades, activities, routine), deadlines, study sessions, quiz results and chats with the assistant.
  • Files you add: text read from documents, calendar files and screenshots you upload, so they can be used for your plan and notes.
  • AI usage: how many AI requests you make each day, to apply fair-use limits.

How we use it

Only to run StudyFlow for you: building and updating your timetable, generating notes and quizzes, and keeping your plan in sync across your devices. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase stores your account and plan (servers in Tokyo, Japan).
  • StudyFlow's AI features are not switched on yet. Before they are, we'll update this page to explain which AI provider processes what you send to it.
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub).

Students under 18

StudyFlow is for students aged 13 and over. If you're under 18, please check with a parent or guardian before signing up. Parents can contact us (contact email coming soon) to see or delete their child's data.

Your choices

You can change your answers at any time in StudyFlow. You can delete your account from the You page, which permanently deletes your plan, quizzes, chats and uploaded text.

Keeping it safe

Your plan is stored so that only your account can read it. Connections are encrypted. The AI key and other secrets stay on our servers.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
