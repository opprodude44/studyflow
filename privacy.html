Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

StudyFlow is in beta. We'll keep this page up to date as it grows, and tell you in the app about any important change.

Who we are

StudyFlow is an independent study planner, built and run by a student developer in Dubai, UAE. To ask a question about your data, use Send feedback on the You page in StudyFlow.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, first name and birth year.
  • Your plan: the answers you give during setup (curriculum, subjects, target grades, activities and routine), your deadlines, study sessions, quiz and flashcard results, and your chats with Flo.
  • Material you add: text read from files you upload, web pages you add by link, and calendar files or links you connect.
  • Usage: how many AI requests you make each day, so we can apply fair-use limits, and any feedback you send us.

We don't collect your location, contacts or anything from your device that you don't add yourself.

How we use it

Only to run StudyFlow for you: building and updating your timetable, answering your questions, making notes, quizzes and flashcards, and keeping your plan in sync across your devices. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.

Services that process your data

  • Supabase stores your account and plan (servers in Tokyo, Japan).
  • Cloudflare runs StudyFlow's server. When you use Flo, the text needed to answer (your message, relevant parts of your plan and any material you've added) is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI, which runs the open AI model that writes the reply. When you add a web page or calendar link, Cloudflare fetches it for you.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website itself.

Flo, the AI

Flo is an AI, not a person. It can make mistakes, so check important facts with your teacher or textbook. Flo won't write coursework (such as an IA, Extended Essay or TOK essay) for you.

Students under 18

StudyFlow is for students aged 13 and over. If you're under 18, please check with a parent or guardian before signing up. A parent or guardian can ask us to see or delete their child's data through Send feedback, or the student can delete the account at any time.

Your choices

You can change your answers at any time. You can permanently delete your account from the You page (Account → Delete account). This removes your plan, quizzes, chats, files and linked calendars.

Keeping it safe

Your plan is stored so that only your account can read it, and connections are encrypted. Secret keys stay on our servers and are never sent to your browser.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
